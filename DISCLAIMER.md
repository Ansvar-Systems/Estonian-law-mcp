## Data Authority and Limitations

### Source Authority

This Tool aggregates data from official Estonian legal databases. However:

| Source Type | Authority Level | Use Case |
|------------|----------------|----------|
| **riigiteataja.ee** | Authoritative (legally binding) | Statute text, consolidated legislation (primary reference) |
| **kohtud.ee** | Official (court records) | Court decisions, Riigikohus rulings |
| **Community/open-data sources** | Supplementary | Cross-references, metadata (verify independently) |
| **EUR-Lex / treaty databases** | Authoritative for EU/international law | EU directives, regulations, international obligations |

**CRITICAL**: Community-maintained resources are **NOT** official government publications. While generally reliable, they may contain transcription errors, outdated information, or incomplete coverage.

### Digital-First Legal System — Riigi Teataja

Estonia's **Riigi Teataja** (riigiteataja.ee) is the sole official source of Estonian legislation and publishes legally binding consolidated texts in digital form. This reflects Estonia's digital-first governance model (e-Estonia). Key points:

- The current consolidated text on riigiteataja.ee is the **only authoritative version** — no separate print gazette takes precedence
- Historical versions (ajaloolised redaktsioonid) are available on the platform with exact applicability dates (jõustumise kuupäev / kehtetuks tunnistamise kuupäev)
- Acts are cited by their short title and RT reference, e.g., Võlaõigusseadus (RT I 2001, 81, 487) or by their RT I number

### GDPR and Estonian Data Protection Implementation

This Tool covers Estonian legislation including the **Isikuandmete kaitse seadus (IKS)** — Estonia's GDPR implementation act — which sets national derogations and supplementary rules. The **Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (AKI, aki.ee)** is the supervisory authority. This Tool is a reference aid and does not constitute legal advice on GDPR or IKS compliance.

### Commercial Legal Databases

For professional legal work, **always cross-check with commercial legal databases** such as:

- Riigi Teataja (riigiteataja.ee — official, free), Juura (juura.ee), JURIDICA (juridica.ee)

These services provide editorial oversight, annotations (kommentaarid), and professional-grade currency guarantees that this Tool **cannot** provide.

## Currency and Staleness

### No Real-Time Updates

- Database updates are **manual** and may lag official publications by **weeks or months**
- Estonian law changes continuously through new acts (seadused), amendments (muudatused), government regulations (määrused), and court decisions (kohtulahendid)
- **Last-updated timestamps** in tool responses indicate data age, but should be treated as unreliable

### Staleness Warnings

The Tool includes staleness warnings when data is >30 days old. **These warnings are not exhaustive**:

- Even "fresh" data may be outdated if recent amendments were published in the Riigi Teataja
- Staleness warnings do not guarantee completeness or accuracy
- Always check official sources for the current legal position (kehtiv õiguslik olukord)

### Amendments and Repeals

- A provision marked "in force" may have been:
  - Amended (muudetud) after the last database update
  - Repealed (kehtetuks tunnistatud) but not yet removed from the database
  - Subject to transitional provisions (üleminekusätted) affecting its applicability
  - Modified by a Riigikohus constitutional review decision (põhiseaduslikkuse järelevalve lahend) not yet reflected in consolidated text
- The Tool does **NOT** automatically track amendments in real-time

## Coverage Gaps

### What's Missing

This Tool does **NOT** include:

1. **Complete case law** — Coverage of Riigikohus (Supreme Court), ringkonnakohtud (circuit courts), and maakohtud (county courts) is limited; do not rely solely on this for case law research (kohtupraktika uuring); use kohtud.ee for court decisions
2. **Historical statute versions** — Limited availability of historical provision wording beyond what riigiteataja.ee exposes; for point-in-time text use the historical versions function on riigiteataja.ee directly
3. **Legal commentary** — No editorial annotations (kommentaarid), academic commentary (õigusteaduslikud artiklid, JURIDICA väljaanded), or practice guides
4. **Unpublished decisions** — Lower court decisions and administrative decisions (haldusaktid, Vabariigi Valitsuse korraldused) largely absent
5. **Full legislative history** — Limited coverage of parliamentary materials (Riigikogu stenogrammid, eelnõud, seletuskirjad, komisjonide protokollid)
6. **Local government law** — Kohaliku omavalitsuse volikogu määrused and other municipal acts are not systematically covered

### Impact on Professional Use

These gaps mean:

- **Incomplete Legal Research**: Tool results are inherently incomplete and may miss critical authorities
- **Context Missing**: Without commentary (kommentaar) and legislative materials (seletuskirjad), interpretation may be incorrect, particularly for newer acts like the äriseadustik (Commercial Code) and tsiviilseadustiku üldosa seadus (General Part of the Civil Code Act)
- **EU Law Blind Spots**: Cannot fully assess EU law obligations or whether Estonian transposition legislation is compliant
